01MS thesis

Burst oscillations in a periodically forced supramolecular system

IISER Thiruvananthapuram · Aug 2025 to Jul 2026 · with Dr. Pushpita Ghosh

A fuel-driven polymerization model that bursts when you shake it at the right rate.

  • Hopf bifurcation
  • Slow–fast analysis
  • Arnold tongues
  • Mathematica
  • Python
Overview and results
  • Model reduction. Reduced a four-variable kinetic model to three variables exactly, using a conservation law, and located its Hopf regimes with eigenvalue analysis.
  • Mechanism. Periodic parametric forcing produces burst oscillations as the trajectory repeatedly crosses a Hopf boundary. Slow–fast analysis gives a burst threshold set by the rate constants rather than by the forcing.
  • Frequency locking. Built Arnold tongue diagrams from Poincaré stroboscopic sections and mapped phase-locking structure, including a devil's staircase.
  • Analytics and space. Derived the Hopf bifurcation surface analytically in Mathematica, then extended the model in space with periodic forcing and Gaussian noise to see pattern formation during assembly and disassembly.
  • Status. Manuscript with Dr. Ghosh to be submitted October 2026.
02Scientific ML

Physics-informed ML that keeps its bifurcations

Self-initiated · Sep 2026 to present

Neural networks that learn dynamics without forgetting where the dynamics change.

  • FiLM conditioning
  • Physical constraints
  • Lyapunov exponents
  • PyTorch
Overview and results
  • Framework. FiLM-conditioned neural networks trained under physical constraints.
  • Benchmarks. Van der Pol, FitzHugh–Nagumo, Lorenz, and the chemically fueled supramolecular oscillator from my thesis.
  • Outcome. Compared with unconstrained neural baselines: better bifurcation accuracy, more stable long-horizon rollouts, closer conservation, and better out-of-distribution generalization.
  • Validation. Jacobian and eigenvalue analysis, Lyapunov exponents, invariant measures, uncertainty quantification, and capacity and ablation studies.
  • Code and reproducibility on GitHub
